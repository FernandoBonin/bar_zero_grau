## Brand & Style

This design system translates the sensorial warmth, nocturnal buzz, and time-honored hospitality of a traditional São Paulo *boteco chique* into an immersive digital discovery interface. Designed for food and drink enthusiasts in Tatuapé, the experience bridges the bustling sociability of an ice-cold draft beer counter (*chope trincando*) with the craft and polish of a contemporary gastrobar.

The visual style blends **Tactile / Atmospheric Warmth** with **Editorial Elegance**:
- Rich, dark-roasted tones mimic polished imbuia wood, cast-iron rechauds, and dark stone counters.
- Radiant golden-amber accents evoke glowing backlight bottles, brass tap handles, and warm filament bar lamps.
- Geometric tile motifs—drawn directly from the bar counter’s iconic crosshatch mosaic—act as subtle watermark borders, separators, and background textures.
- The interface emphasizes appetizing sensory visuals, effortless browsing through classified menu categories (Petiscos, Rechaud, Porções, Drinks Autorais, Chopp), and an intimate bar-room atmosphere.

## Colors

The palette operates in a default rich dark mode, creating the intimate, dimly lit atmosphere of an evening gastrobar:

- **Primary (`#D69A43`) - Golden Amber:** Represents warm bar lighting, crisp fried appetizers (*pastéis* and *bolinhos*), and chilled draught beer. Used for primary CTAs, active tab indicators, badges, price highlights, and key decorative motifs.
- **Secondary (`#873D32`) - Deep Terracotta / Malbec:** Evokes sizzling cuts on cast-iron grills, wine reductions, and cocktail garnishes. Employed for chef specials, temperature tags, house recommendations, and high-impact category accents.
- **Tertiary (`#483124`) - Roasted Wood:** Anchors cards, surface containers, navigation drawers, and segmented controls, mimicking dark polished timber countertops.
- **Neutral (`#20201E`) - Deep Charcoal Smoke:** The canvas foundation. Subtly tinted warm black that prevents harsh digital contrast while allowing food photography and golden typography to glow.
- **Surface Tones & Textures:** 
  - Canvas: `#161614`
  - Elevated Container / Cards: `#25221F`
  - High Container / Modals: `#322C27`
  - Text Primary: `#FFFBF4` (Warm Cream White)
  - Text Secondary: `#C9BFB5` (Parchment Mist)
  - Border Subtle: `rgba(214, 154, 67, 0.18)`

## Typography

The type system blends the traditional editorial authority of São Paulo culinary culture with crisp, ergonomic legibility on hand-held screens:

- **Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Used for venue branding, section intros, menu category headers, and featured culinary dish names. Its high contrast, expressive serifs, and classic proportions pay tribute to vintage menu typography and upscale brass-trimmed bars.
- **Body, UI & Prices (`Work Sans`):** Clean, geometric, and grounded. It maintains flawless clarity when reading Portuguese dish descriptions, ingredient lists, allergy tags, and beverage ABV notes under dim ambient conditions.
- **Labeling Style:** Category badges, dietary chips, and pricing labels utilize slight tracking (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) in medium-to-bold weights, echoing embossed leather menu tags.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first browsing architecture designed to simulate an intuitive, hand-held physical menu board:

- **Mobile Viewport (< 640px):** Single-column vertical scroll with sticky horizontal category pills at the top. Grid columns: 4; `margin`: `1rem`; `gutter`: `0.75rem`. Cards run edge-to-edge with inset margins or full bleed with internal content padding.
- **Tablet / Counter Device (640px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with 2-column menu grids. Sticky category rail or split view (categories on left, items on right). `margin`: `1.5rem`; `gutter`: `1rem`.
- **Desktop / Display Screen (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of `1200px` centered. Ideal for venue showcases, event bookings, and panoramic photo displays.
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Generous vertical breathing room between culinary groupings (`space-xl`), while maintaining tight, cohesive coupling between a dish's title, description, and price tag (`space-xs` to `space-sm`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through **Tonal Warmth, Backlit Glows, and Textured Tile Framing**:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Deep matte charcoal (`#161614`) with an ultra-faint SVG geometric watermark inspired by the bar counter’s four-point star/crosshatch mosaic.
- **Layer 1 (Card & Containers):** `#25221F` warm wood charcoal. Outlined with a fine `1px` translucent gold border (`rgba(214, 154, 67, 0.2)`). Shadows are softly tinted with amber warmth: `0 4px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4), 0 1px 3px rgba(214, 154, 67, 0.05)`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating & Sticky Chrome):** Sticky category bar and top navigation adopt a frosted dark wood glassmorphism: `background: rgba(32, 32, 30, 0.88)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, with a lower hairline border of `rgba(214, 154, 67, 0.3)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Dish Previews):** `#322C27` elevated surface with dramatic bottom-sheet projection: `0 -12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Ambient Lighting Cue:** Hero sections and key callouts feature subtle radial gradient blooms behind cocktail and food imagery (`radial-gradient(circle, rgba(214,154,67,0.18) 0%, transparent 70%)`), replicating warm backbar pendant illumination.

## Shapes

The design embraces a **Soft Architectural (`roundedness: 1`)** geometry that balances heritage craftsmanship with modern elegance:

- Standard controls, cards, and input fields utilize a tight, structured `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.5rem` (8px) radius, referencing polished stone tiles and framed wood menus.
- Badges and micro-tags stay crisp with `0.25rem` corners.
- Category filtering chips and circular action buttons (such as quick-filter toggles or image zoom buttons) utilize full pill-shapes (`rounded-full`) to differentiate interactive touch targets from content cards.
- Dish photography incorporates subtle geometric corners with optional classic inner filigree or tile-motif borders on hero items.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Call / Location / Share):** Golden amber background (`#D69A43`), dark text (`#161614`, font weight 600), soft rectangular shape (`rounded: 0.375rem`), with a soft amber hover/press glow.
- **Secondary Action:** Outlined in antique brass (`rgba(214, 154, 67, 0.5)`), transparent background, cream typography (`#FFFBF4`).
- **Ghost Action:** No border, cream text with underline transition and icon shift.

### Chips & Category Tabs
- **Sticky Menu Filter Pills:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`). Inactive state is transparent charcoal with a fine `1px` border (`rgba(255, 251, 244, 0.12)`) and light parchment text. Active state transitions to solid warm amber with dark roasted typography, instantly confirming the current category.
- **Dietary & Attribute Tags:** Miniature rectangular tags (`rounded: 0.25rem`) with semi-transparent tinted backgrounds: Terracotta tint for *"Carro-chefe / Especialidade"*, Forest Sage for vegetarian options, and Golden Amber for *"Para Compartilhar"*.

### Menu Cards (Food & Drinks)
- **Compact List Card (Quick Scanning):** Two-column horizontal orientation. Left side contains dish name in `Playfair Display`, concise description, portion/servings metadata, and golden price tag. Right side houses a high-definition, cropped square thumbnail (`80x80px` or `96x96px`) with soft rounded corners.
- **Featured Hero Card (Signature Rechauds & Cocktails):** Full-bleed photography on top with a warm dark gradient overlay, followed by editorial typography, terracotta badges, and an ornate tile motif separator between items.

### Lists & Dividers
- Menu items are delimited by a bespoke thin divider featuring a center diamond or tile node, referencing the iconic bar counter pattern. Line style: `1px solid rgba(214, 154, 67, 0.15)`.

### Search & Filtering Bar
- Recessed input field with dark roasted fill (`#1A1917`), subtle warm cream placeholder text, amber magnifying glass icon, and clear button. Instant client-side filtering without form submission.

### Drawer & Bottom Sheets
- Mobile detail sheets pop up from the bottom with a brass grab handle, displaying high-res photos of dishes (like Picanha no Rechaud or Pastéis sortidos), origin of cuts, pairing recommendations (e.g., suggested beer or caipirinha), and allergens.
